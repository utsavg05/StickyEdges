# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want the Sticky Edges community to be welcoming, respectful, and safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas on their merits.
- Welcome questions and help others learn.
- Respect differing opinions and lived experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/utsavg05/StickyEdges/security/advisories/new). Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
